Wildlife to Look For

Lions

Lions are one of Africa's most recognizable predators. They can often be spotted resting during the heat of the day.

Elephants

African elephants are found in several South African parks and are known for their intelligence, family groups and impressive size.

Rhinos

South Africa is home to both black and white rhinoceroses. Visitors should always observe these animals from a safe distance.

Leopards

Leopards are powerful and elusive cats. They are often difficult to spot because they are excellent at hiding among vegetation.

Protecting South Africa's Wildlife

National parks play an important role in protecting wildlife and natural habitats. Responsible tourism helps support conservation efforts and local communities. Visitors can help by following park rules, keeping wildlife at a safe distance, avoiding littering and respecting the natural environment.
