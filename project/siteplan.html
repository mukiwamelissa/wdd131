🌿 Explore South Africa National Parks

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Explore South Africa National Parks

This name reflects the purpose of the website, which is to help visitors discover South Africa's beautiful national parks.

Optional Domain: exploresaparks.com

Site Purpose

This website provides information about South Africa's national parks, including wildlife, attractions, visitor activities, travel tips, accommodation, and conservation.

Scenarios

  • Which national park is best for seeing the Big Five?
  • How much does it cost to visit Kruger National Park?

Color Schema

  • Forest Green (#2E8B57) – Header, navigation, footer, buttons.
  • Sand Beige (#F5E6C8) – Background.
  • Golden Yellow (#D4A017) – Headings and links.

Typography

  • Montserrat – Headings
  • Open Sans – Body text
  • Merriweather – Logo and special titles
