Plan Your Visit

Planning ahead can make your national park experience safer, more comfortable and more enjoyable.

Best Time to Visit

The best time to visit depends on the park and the experience you want. The dry winter months can make wildlife easier to spot in many game reserves because animals gather around available water sources.

What to Bring

  • Comfortable clothing
  • Walking shoes
  • Sun protection
  • Drinking water
  • Binoculars
  • Camera

Safety Tips

  • Stay inside designated areas.
  • Never feed wild animals.
  • Keep a safe distance from wildlife.
  • Follow park rules and signs.
  • Keep emergency contact information available.
